## Why precision is below 0.90 (user asked for a full diagnosis)
- To reach 0.90 gated: Conduit must drop from 11 to at most 8 confirmed false alarms (78 true); BookStack from 21 to at most 2 (19 true, about 4 per run).
- **Confirmed false alarms by cause:**
  - Conduit: intent-name I-create/I-edit 9 (login 5), R12 1, R11 favourite button 1.
  - BookStack: intent-name 11 (login 9), hidden `/link` anchor 3, activity feed and renamed shelf 3, misreads 4 (R10 tag search, filter checkbox, G2 time drift, GET search as I-submit).
- **Precision by tier, confirmed:**
  - Conduit: T1 24/33, T2 43/45, implicit 11/11.
  - BookStack: T1 15/29, T2 4/10.
  - T1 is supposed to be the trusted tier; it is the worst, because I-create/I-edit are T1. T1 is also never sent to the judge.
- **Root causes found in the code:**
  1. The intent object comes from the model's intent text; typed field values are used only when it is empty (`consistency.py` ~L928). Only the verb `login` is skipped, so "create 'session'" (typ auth) goes through.
  2. R09 skips event feeds (`EVENT_FEED`) for added items only, not removed ones (`consistency.py` L1142 vs L1145-1153).
  3. R02 probes hidden anchors.
  4. There is no volatility model for relative times, search and filter forms, or tag-to-search links.
  5. The gate tests reproducibility, and these false alarms are deterministic, so they replay 3/3. It rejected only 7 of 18 (Conduit) and 11 of 32 (BookStack).
  6. Process gap: the v4 lock was judged on resweeps of v3 runs, which carry no intents, so I-create/I-edit were never exercised before the lock, and the pilot findings were not labelled.
- **Development estimate on these runs** (not a result):
  - Conduit: fixing (1) alone gives about 97%.
  - BookStack: fixing (1)-(3) gives about 79%; reaching 0.90 needs most of the misreads fixed too.
- Added to I49.
